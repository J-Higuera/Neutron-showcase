25-36 / The studio side

Studio notes / access

Shown near the conditions that produced the work.

48 Studio keeps the gallery wall close to the tables, sinks, crates, frames, measuring tape, and notes that shape the exhibition. Visitors can read the installation as a finished room and as a working sequence.

  • Studio visits by appointment with artists and director.
  • Artist notes paired with selected works and wall labels.
  • Workshops for works on paper, framing, and conservation handling.
  • Critique evenings held after hours for local artists.
  • Installation access during the final week before opening.
