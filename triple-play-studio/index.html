Evidence driven

Every sprint has a scoreboard.

The work is judged by what became clearer, what shipped, and what the next decision can safely rest on.

  1. Frame

    Map business pressure, user jobs, technical constraints, and the fastest proof worth building.

  2. Prototype

    Make the riskiest part real enough to test with users, teams, or systems before the full build.

  3. Ship

    Move into production with telemetry, release notes, support paths, and ownership already assigned.

  4. Compound

    Turn adoption signals into the next tranche of work, preserving momentum without bloating scope.
