01the blind estate

Your estate is already a graph. Nobody can see it.

Every enterprise runs on a web of dependencies — services leaning on identities, identities on directories, pipelines on queues, controls on assumptions. The picture exists only in fragments: a wiki diagram from two re-orgs ago, a spreadsheet of servers, the one engineer who remembers why the firewall rule exists.

  • Change is blind. Every migration begins with archaeology.
  • Controls drift. What is enforced diverges from what is written.
  • Risk pools in the seams — between teams, clouds and eras.
